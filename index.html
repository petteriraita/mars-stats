Drafting and metrics

Leave the draft-generation field empty for the starting hand. Gen 2 is the first normal four-card research draft; later generations follow in order. “Drafted” means actively selected during the rotating draft and “Bought” means included in the final purchase.

Draft-card data in the public export generally contains the three explicit selections; the automatically received fourth card is not identified there. Metrics are hidden below the chosen observation threshold.

Displayed starting-hand and draft performance values are smoothed: 60% selected decision point, 20% each adjacent point. At an edge, the available neighbor has 25% weight. Offer counts remain exact.

In Corp + Card, “Adjust Elo for corporation” subtracts that corporation's average Elo gain for the selected maps, Prelude setting, and table-Elo cohort. It affects both outcomes, sorting, card details, and copied AI context; it does not change counts or rates.
